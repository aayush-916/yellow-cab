    Pricing

    How your rate is worked out

    Four things make up every outstation fare. We tell you all four before you book, so the final figure is never a surprise.

    01

    Per Km Rate

    Every car has its own per-km rate. Sedan ₹13, Ertiga ₹15, Scorpio ₹17, Innova Crysta ₹22 on round trips.

    02

    Minimum 200 Km

    Round trips are billed for at least 200 km, even if you travel less.

    03

    Driver Charge

    A ₹200 driver charge is added on outstation round trips.

    04

    Toll, Parking & Food

    Toll tax, parking and driver food are paid as they come. Nothing added on top.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    What cars do you have?

    Mini (hatchback), Sedan, Ertiga, Scorpio and Innova Crysta, plus Fortuner and decorated cars for weddings on request.

    What does the local package include?

    For trips within Patna only: 8 hours and 80 km with a driver. Mini ₹2,000, Sedan ₹2,500, SUV (Ertiga / Scorpio) ₹3,500 and Innova Crysta ₹4,500. Toll, parking and driver food are extra.

    How is an outstation round trip charged?

    Per km: Sedan ₹13, Ertiga ₹15, Scorpio ₹17, Innova Crysta ₹22. Minimum 200 km, plus ₹200 driver charge, toll tax, parking and driver food.

    Do you do one-way drops?

    Yes. ₹20/km for a Sedan and ₹26/km for an Ertiga, plus toll. You pay for one side only.

    Are toll and parking included?

    No. Toll tax, parking and driver food are paid separately as they come, on every trip type.

    Is a driver included?

    Yes. Every car comes with a driver, on every trip.

    Do I need to pay an advance to get a rate?

    No. Send your trip details on WhatsApp or call 9263220075 and we share the fare first.

    When can I call you?

    Any time. We are open 24 hours, 7 days a week, including airport and station pickups at night.
